! Title: tamescroll — Twitch
! Description: Removes Twitch's discovery surfaces — the auto-playing
!              front-page carousel, the home page's recommended shelves,
!              recommended channels in the side bar and the Browse menu —
!              while leaving channels you open, Following and search.
! Version: 1
! Expires: 1 day
! License: CC0-1.0 (see rules/LICENSE)
!
! VERIFIED LIVE 2026-11-23, www.twitch.tv desktop, signed out, images
! blurred. data-a-target / data-test-selector are Twitch's own test hooks.

!surface: home Home recommendations
! The home page: auto-playing featured stream carousel, "Live on Twitch",
! "Categories we think you'll like" and the rest of the shelves. The page
! is the one that holds the carousel, so the whole scroller goes with it.
twitch.tv##[data-a-target="front-page-carousel"]
twitch.tv##[data-a-target="root-scroller"]:has([data-a-target="front-page-carousel"])
twitch.tv##[data-a-target="root-scroller"]:has([aria-label="Live Channels"])

!surface: side_recs Recommended channels
! Side bar cards Twitch picks for you; followed channels stay.
twitch.tv##[data-test-selector="recommended-channel"]

!surface: browse Browse menu
twitch.tv##[data-a-target="browse-link"]

!surface: promoted Promoted and upsells
twitch.tv##[data-a-target="prime-offers-icon"]
twitch.tv##[class*="upsell-bottom-banner"]
