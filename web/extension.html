Where it works
YouTubeHome feed, Shorts, previews and the Explore menu hidden. A short opens as a normal video, and autoplay stops at the end.
Redditr/popular, r/all, News and Explore hidden, and posts from communities you haven't joined.
XThe For you tab, trends and "Who to follow" hidden.
InstagramExplore, Reels and suggested posts from accounts you don't follow hidden.
TikTokThe For You feed, Explore and LIVE hidden, and the endless swipe stopped.
TwitchHome recommendations, recommended channels and Browse hidden.
LinkedInLinkedIn News and posts from people you don't follow hidden.
Facebook"Log in to see more" pop-ups hidden on public pages when you're signed out.
How it feels
01

On from the start

Install it and the sites are already calm. Nothing to set up.

02

Bring back what you want

Every hidden part has its own switch, per site. A green dot on the icon means a page is tamed.

03

Blur, if you like

Optional picture blur on each site, in three strengths.

H
